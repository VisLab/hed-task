# Response inhibition and stopping tasks

A prepared or prepotent response must be withheld or cancelled on a minority of trials, or withheld throughout; commission errors, stopping latency and breaks of fixation are the measures.

This family contains 5 tasks.

## [Anti-Saccade](../hedtsk_anti_saccade.md)

Participants must suppress a prepotent saccade toward a peripheral cue and generate a voluntary saccade to the opposite location; errors and saccade latency index oculomotor inhibition. ## [Fixate](../hedtsk_fixate.md)

Participant holds gaze on a fixation target. It is used in two ways: as a block of its own with nothing else to do, as a baseline or control, and as a job held throughout another task so that the eyes stay still while that task is performed. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-sustained-attention">Sustained attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.html#hed-goal-maintenance">Goal maintenance</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-response-inhibition">Response inhibition</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>Fixation Block</span><span>Fixation Baseline</span><span>Central Fixation</span></span></span>, no variants, also filed under <span class="pop" tabindex="0">2 other families<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../families/attentional_cueing.html">Attentional cueing and orienting tasks</a></span><span><a href="../families/visual_search_and_tracking.html">Visual search and tracking tasks</a></span></span></span>.
