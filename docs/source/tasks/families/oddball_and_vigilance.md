# Oddball, vigilance and continuous performance tasks

A long stream of stimuli contains rare targets or deviants; detection, false alarms, response variability or the evoked response to the rare event are the measures.

This family contains 6 tasks and cross-lists 1 more.

## [AX Continuous Performance](../hedtsk_ax_continuous_performance.md)

Letters come in cue-probe pairs and the target is an X only when it follows an A; errors on the pairs that break the rule index use of the held cue. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">8 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-sustained-attention">Sustained attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-alerting">Alerting</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-response-inhibition">Response inhibition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/awareness_agency_and_metacognition.html#hed-mind-wandering">Spontaneous thought</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.html#hed-goal-maintenance">Goal maintenance</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-proactive-control">Proactive control</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/short_term_and_working_memory.html#hed-active-maintenance">Active maintenance</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">2 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>AX-CPT</span><span>AX-CPT (Context Processing)</span></span></span>, no variants.

## [Continuous Performance](../hedtsk_continuous_performance.md)

Extended stream of stimuli in which a rare target requires a response; omission and commission errors index sustained attention and response control. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-sustained-attention">Sustained attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-alerting">Alerting</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-response-inhibition">Response inhibition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/awareness_agency_and_metacognition.html#hed-mind-wandering">Spontaneous thought</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>CPT</span><span>CPT-II</span><span>Continuous Performance</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">6 variants<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">Identical Pairs CPT (IP-CPT)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">Gradual-Onset CPT (gradCPT)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">Auditory CPT</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">Conners&#x27; CPT-3</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">TOVA (Test of Variables of Attention)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_continuous_performance.html#variants">CPT with Emotional Distractors</a></span></span></span>.

## [Mismatch Negativity](../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.md)

Passive auditory oddball in which rare deviant sounds elicit a negative ERP around 150-250 ms, indexing pre-attentive auditory change detection. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">4 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.html#hed-auditory-perception">Auditory perception</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.html#hed-auditory-tone-discrimination">Auditory tone discrimination</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.html#hed-pitch-perception">Pitch perception</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.html#hed-acoustic-processing">Acoustic processing</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>MMN</span><span>Mismatch Negativity Paradigm</span><span>Mismatch Negativity</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 variants<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.html#variants">Location/Spatial MMN</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.html#variants">Complex Pattern MMN</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.html#variants">Multi-Feature MMN (Optimum)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.html#variants">Phoneme/Speech MMN</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_mismatch_negativity.html#variants">Visual MMN (vMMN)</a></span></span></span>.

## [Oddball](../hedtsk_oddball.md)

Sequence of standards with rare deviants or targets; deviants elicit a large positive ERP around 300 ms (P3a/P3b), indexing attention and context updating. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-temporal-attention">Temporal attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-attentional-capture">Attentional capture</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-sustained-attention">Sustained attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/short_term_and_working_memory.html#hed-working-memory-updating">Working memory updating</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>P300 Task</span><span>Auditory Oddball</span><span>Visual Oddball</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">8 variants<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Three-Stimulus Novelty Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Visual Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Passive Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Active Counting vs. Button-Press Response</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Duration Deviance Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Cross-Modal Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Emotional Oddball</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_oddball.html#variants">Roving Paradigm</a></span></span></span>.

## [Psychomotor Vigilance](../hedtsk_psychomotor_vigilance.md)

Simple reaction to visual or auditory stimuli appearing at random intervals over 5-10 minutes; mean RT, lapses, and false starts index sustained attention. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">4 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-sustained-attention">Sustained attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-alerting">Alerting</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/motor_preparation_timing_and_execution.html#hed-response-execution">Response execution</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/awareness_agency_and_metacognition.html#hed-mind-wandering">Spontaneous thought</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">2 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>PVT</span><span>Psychomotor Vigilance</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 variants<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_psychomotor_vigilance.html#variants">Brief PVT (PVT-B)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_psychomotor_vigilance.html#variants">Palm PVT / Mobile PVT</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_psychomotor_vigilance.html#variants">Auditory PVT</a></span></span></span>.
