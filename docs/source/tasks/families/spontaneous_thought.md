# Spontaneous thought tasks

No external task directs thought, or thought is sampled as it drifts from an ongoing task; the recording during the unoccupied interval, or the participant's reports of what they were thinking, is the measure.

This family contains 1 tasks and cross-lists 1 more.

## [Rest](../hedtsk_rest.md)

Participant stays awake, still and unoccupied for a block of several minutes while spontaneous activity is recorded; the block is a baseline for task blocks or a resting-state recording in its own right.
