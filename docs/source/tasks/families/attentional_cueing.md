# Attentional cueing and orienting tasks

A cue precedes a target and predicts its location or identity with some validity; the benefit of valid over invalid cues is the measure.

This family contains 3 tasks.

## [Attention Network](../hedtsk_attention_network.md)

Identify the direction of a central arrow flanked by congruent or incongruent arrows, preceded by various cue types; RT differences across cue and flanker conditions index three independent attention networks (alerting, orienting, executive). Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">6 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-alerting">Alerting</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-orienting">Orienting</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-executive-attention">Executive attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-interference-control">Interference control</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-conflict-monitoring">Conflict monitoring</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">3 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>Attention Network Test</span><span>ANT</span><span>Clinical Screening ANT</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">6 variations<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">Standard ANT (Fan et al., 2002)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">ANT-I (Callejas et al.)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">ANT-R (Revised)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">Lateralized ANT (LANT)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">Child ANT</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_attention_network.html#variations">ANT with Emotional Stimuli</a></span></span></span>, also filed under <span class="pop" tabindex="0">1 other family<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../families/conflict_and_interference.html">Conflict and interference tasks</a></span></span></span>.

## [Dot-Probe](../hedtsk_dot_probe.md)

A pair of cues (often threat/neutral) is followed by a probe at one cue location; RT difference by cue type indexes attentional bias. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-spatial-attention">Spatial attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-emotion-recognition">Emotion recognition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-attentional-capture">Attentional capture</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-orienting">Orienting</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">2 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>Visual Probe Task</span><span>Attentional Probe</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">6 variations<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Standard Face Dot-Probe</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Word Dot-Probe</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Pictorial Dot-Probe</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Detection vs. Classification Probe</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Subliminal/Masked Dot-Probe</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_dot_probe.html#variations">Positive Dot-Probe</a></span></span></span>.
