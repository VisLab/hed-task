# Emotion elicitation and regulation tasks

Affective stimuli are presented to elicit, recognize or regulate an emotional response; ratings, physiological responses and recognition accuracy are the measures.

This family contains 4 tasks.

## [Affective Picture Viewing](../hedtsk_affective_picture_viewing.md)

Passive or instructed viewing of emotionally valenced images (IAPS, GAPED, NAPS, OASIS) while physiological, neural, or rating responses to valence and arousal are recorded. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">6 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-emotion-recognition">Emotion recognition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-emotion-regulation">Emotion regulation</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/face_and_object_perception.html#hed-visual-perception">Visual perception</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/reward_anticipation_and_motivation.html#hed-approach-motivation">Approach motivation</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/reward_anticipation_and_motivation.html#hed-avoidance-motivation">Avoidance motivation</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">2 aliases<span class="pop-card"><span>IAPS Viewing</span><span>Passive Viewing Task</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">1 variation<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_picture_viewing.html#variations">Category-Specific Selection</a></span></span></span>.

## [Affective Priming](../hedtsk_affective_priming.md)

Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-affective-priming">Affective priming</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-emotion-recognition">Emotion recognition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/language_comprehension_and_production.html#hed-semantic-processing">Semantic processing</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/motor_preparation_timing_and_execution.html#hed-response-selection">Response selection</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-response-conflict">Response conflict</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">1 alias<span class="pop-card"><span>Evaluative Priming</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">7 variations<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Evaluative Decision (Good/Bad)</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Pronunciation/Naming</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Subliminal Priming</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Picture-Word Priming</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Face-Face Priming</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Cross-Modal Affective Priming</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_affective_priming.html#variations">Masked vs. Unmasked Priming</a></span></span></span>, also filed under <span class="pop" tabindex="0">1 other family<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../families/conflict_and_interference.html">Conflict and interference tasks</a></span></span></span>.

## [Emotion Regulation](../hedtsk_emotion_regulation.md)
