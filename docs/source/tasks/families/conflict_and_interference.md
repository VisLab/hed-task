# Conflict and interference tasks

A task-relevant feature must be reported while a task-irrelevant feature of the same stimulus or of a distractor pulls toward a competing response; the cost on incongruent trials is the measure.

This family contains 8 tasks and cross-lists 2 more.

## [Emotional Stroop](../hedtsk_emotional_stroop.md)

Color-naming of emotionally valenced words; RT slowing on threat-related words indexes attentional bias to affective content. Engages <span class="pop" tabindex="0">5 processes<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-selective-attention">Selective attention</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-interference-control">Interference control</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/emotion_perception_and_regulation.html#hed-emotion-recognition">Emotion recognition</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/selective_and_sustained_attention.html#hed-attentional-capture">Attentional capture</a></span><span><a href="../../processes/inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.html#hed-conflict-monitoring">Conflict monitoring</a></span></span></span>. <span class="pop" tabindex="0">1 alias<span class="pop-card"><span>Affective Stroop</span></span></span>, <span class="pop" tabindex="0">4 variants<span class="pop-card"><span><a href="../hedtsk_emotional_stroop.html#variants">Disorder-Specific Variants</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_emotional_stroop.html#variants">Pictorial Emotional Stroop</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_emotional_stroop.html#variants">Subliminal Emotional Stroop</a></span><span><a href="../hedtsk_emotional_stroop.html#variants">Face-Word Emotional Stroop</a></span></span></span>.
