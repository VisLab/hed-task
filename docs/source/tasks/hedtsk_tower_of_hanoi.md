# Tower of Hanoi Task

**HED task ID:** `hedtsk_tower_of_hanoi`

**Family:** [Rule use, planning and reasoning tasks](families/executive_and_reasoning.md)

**Also known as:** Tower of Hanoi

Move a stack of graded disks to another peg one at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one; moves and time index planning.

## Description

A puzzle with disks of different sizes on three pegs. All disks must be moved to another peg, one at a time, without ever placing a larger disk on a smaller one. More moves are needed than in the Tower of London, and the rule about sizes must be kept in mind.

## Inclusion test

An experiment is an instance of this task when its procedure matches, it manipulates at
least one of the listed variables, and it records at least one of the listed measures.

```{list-table}
:widths: 15 85
:header-rows: 0

* - **Procedure**
  - 1. Disks of graded sizes are stacked on one of three pegs.
    2. Participants move the disks one at a time to another peg, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one, in as few moves as possible.
* - **Manipulations**
  - Number of disks
* - **Measurements**
  - - Number of moves
```
