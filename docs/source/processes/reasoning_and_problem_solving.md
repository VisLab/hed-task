## Deductive reasoning

**Process ID:** `hed_deductive_reasoning`

**Also known as:** **Logical reasoning** - Broader term encompassing deductive and inductive forms; its definition is a union of the two, and the Wason Selection Task links here.

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Wason Selection Task](../tasks/hedtsk_wason_selection.md)

**Fundamental references**

## Insight

**Process ID:** `hed_insight`

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Remote Associates Task](../tasks/hedtsk_remote_associates.md)

(hed-means-ends-analysis)=
## Means-ends analysis

**Process ID:** `hed_means_ends_analysis`

Iterative problem-solving strategy in which the distance between the current state and the goal state is reduced by selecting operators that diminish the largest remaining difference.

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Tower of London Task](../tasks/hedtsk_tower_of_london.md)

**Fundamental references**

- Newell A & Simon HA (1972) *Human Problem Solving* Prentice-Hall
- Atwood ME & Polson PG (1976) *Cognitive Psychology* 8:191-216 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(76)90023-2))

**Further references**

- Huys QJM et al. (2015) *PLoS Computational Biology* 11:e1004112

## Subgoaling

**Process ID:** `hed_subgoaling`

Decomposition of a goal into an ordered sequence of subgoals whose satisfaction advances the overall task; dissociable from means-ends search and often studied through hierarchical task analysis.

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Tower of London Task](../tasks/hedtsk_tower_of_london.md)
