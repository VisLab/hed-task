## [Associative Learning and Reinforcement](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md)

Learning of stimulus-stimulus, stimulus-response, and action-outcome contingencies under reinforcement; Pavlovian and instrumental conditioning; extinction; reversal; model-based and model-free reinforcement learning; reward prediction error.

### [Associative learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-associative-learning)

Learning of co-occurrence relations between stimuli or between stimuli and responses. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Extinction](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-extinction)

Decrease in a previously reinforced response when reinforcement is withheld; a form of new inhibitory learning rather than erasure. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Goal-directed behavior](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-goal-directed-behavior)

Behavior that is sensitive to current outcome value, characteristic of action–outcome learning. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Habit](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-habit)

Behavior that is insensitive to the current value of its outcome, characteristic of stimulus–response learning. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Instrumental conditioning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-instrumental-conditioning)

Learning that an action produces an outcome; also called operant conditioning. Encompasses both goal-directed (action–outcome) and habitual (stimulus–response) control, studied via reinforcement schedules and outcome-devaluation procedures. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Model-based learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-model-based-learning)

Reinforcement learning that uses an internal model of the environment's transition and reward structure to plan. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Model-free learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-model-free-learning)

Reinforcement learning from cached value estimates updated by prediction errors, without an explicit model of the environment. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Pavlovian conditioning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-pavlovian-conditioning)

Learning that a neutral stimulus predicts a biologically significant outcome, leading to conditioned responding. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Policy learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-policy-learning)

Direct learning of a mapping from states to actions without necessarily estimating values. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Reinforcement learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-reinforcement-learning)

Learning to select actions that maximize cumulative reward through experience with reward prediction errors. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Reversal learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-reversal-learning)

Relearning after contingencies between stimuli (or responses) and outcomes are switched. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Reward prediction error](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-reward-prediction-error)

Signed difference between received and expected reward, instantiated by phasic midbrain dopamine firing. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Value learning](associative_learning_and_reinforcement.md#hed-value-learning)

Acquisition of the expected value of stimuli, actions, or states from experience with outcomes. Engaged by 2 tasks.

## [Auditory and Pre-Attentive Deviance Processing](auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.md)

Pitch perception, auditory tone discrimination, acoustic processing, auditory perception; the family of processes indexed by MMN / oddball paradigms.

### [Acoustic processing](auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.md#hed-acoustic-processing)

Low-level analysis of sound, including frequency, intensity, and temporal structure. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Auditory perception](auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.md#hed-auditory-perception)

Perception of sound, including pitch, timbre, loudness, and spatial location. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Auditory tone discrimination](auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.md#hed-auditory-tone-discrimination)

Judgment of whether two tones differ in pitch, intensity, or duration. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Pitch perception](auditory_and_pre_attentive_deviance_processing.md#hed-pitch-perception)

Perception of the highness or lowness of a sound, related to fundamental frequency. Engaged by 1 task.

## [Awareness, Agency, and Metacognition](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md)

Conscious experience of perception (perceptual awareness, masking); metacognitive monitoring and control; sense of agency; body ownership; interoceptive awareness; self-referential processing; feeling of knowing; judgment of learning; mind wandering; self-monitoring; attentional awareness.

### [Attentional awareness](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-attentional-awareness)

Awareness of the current focus and content of attention. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Body ownership](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-body-ownership)

The experience that a body or body-part belongs to the self, revealed by multisensory illusions in which synchronous visuo-tactile or visuo-proprioceptive input shifts the perceived location of the body. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Feeling of knowing](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-feeling-of-knowing)

Judgment that information currently not retrievable would be recognized if presented. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Interoceptive awareness](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-interoceptive-awareness)

Conscious perception of internal bodily signals such as heartbeat, respiration, and visceral state; indexed by heartbeat-detection accuracy, heartbeat-evoked potentials, and interoceptive sensibility measures. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Judgment of learning](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-judgment-of-learning)

Prediction of future memory performance made during or after encoding. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Masking](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-masking)

Reduction in visibility or detectability of a target stimulus by a temporally or spatially adjacent masker. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Metacognitive control](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-metacognitive-control)

Regulation of cognition based on metacognitive monitoring - study-time allocation, strategy selection, answer withholding, and restart decisions - linking second-order evaluation to first-order behavior. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Metacognitive monitoring](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-metacognitive-monitoring)

Second-order evaluation of ongoing first-order cognition - how confident one is in a perception, memory, or judgment - yielding reportable assessments that dissociate from first-order accuracy. Engaged by 6 tasks.

### [Mind wandering](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-mind-wandering)

Task-unrelated thought that arises during an ongoing task. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Perceptual awareness](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-perceptual-awareness)

Conscious access to perceptual content. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Self-monitoring](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-self-monitoring)

Ongoing evaluation of one's own performance against task goals and expected outcomes. Engaged by 6 tasks.

### [Self-referential processing](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-self-referential-processing)

Processing of information in relation to the self, yielding enhanced encoding for self-relevant material and characteristic engagement of cortical midline structures (mPFC, PCC). Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Sense of agency](awareness_agency_and_metacognition.md#hed-sense-of-agency)

The experience of being the cause of one's own actions and their sensory consequences, dissociable into implicit (intentional binding) and explicit (authorship judgment) components. Engaged by 1 task.

## [Cognitive Flexibility and Higher-Order Executive Function](cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.md)

Task-set reconfiguration (set shifting), rule updating, strategy selection, goal maintenance. Current inhabitants: goal maintenance, set shifting, strategy use.

### [Goal maintenance](cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.md#hed-goal-maintenance)

Active holding of task goals, rules, or sub-goals in a form that biases ongoing processing. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Set shifting](cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.md#hed-set-shifting)

Flexible reconfiguration of the task set in response to a change in task demands; the primary experimental operationalization of cognitive flexibility, measured by task-switching paradigms and the WCST. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Strategy use](cognitive_flexibility_and_higher_order_executive_function.md#hed-strategy-use)

Selection and implementation of a cognitive procedure chosen to improve performance on a task. Engaged by 6 tasks.

## [Emotion Perception and Regulation](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md)

Recognition of emotional signals (face, voice, body), affective priming, cognitive reappraisal, expressive suppression, emotion regulation strategies.

### [Affective priming](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md#hed-affective-priming)

Facilitation or interference in evaluating a target by a valence-related prime. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Cognitive reappraisal](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md#hed-cognitive-reappraisal)

Reinterpretation of an emotional stimulus to change its affective impact. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Emotion recognition](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md#hed-emotion-recognition)

Identification of emotional states from cues such as facial expression or vocal prosody. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Emotion regulation](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md#hed-emotion-regulation)

Processes by which individuals influence which emotions they have, when, and how they experience and express them. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Expressive suppression](emotion_perception_and_regulation.md#hed-expressive-suppression)

Inhibition of outward behavioral expression of emotion. Engaged by 1 task.

## [Face and Object Perception](face_and_object_perception.md)

Processes that recover identity, category, or structure from sensory form: face identity, face perception, visual object recognition, visual form recognition, visual perception, pattern recognition, motion perception (including biological motion), depth perception; plus the non-visual object-perception rows (somatosensory, gustatory, olfactory, proprioceptive) that have no better home.

### [Biological motion perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-biological-motion-perception)

Recognition of animate motion from sparse kinematic cues, such as point-light displays. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Depth perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-depth-perception)

Perception of distance and three-dimensional structure from monocular and binocular cues. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Face identity recognition](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-face-identity-recognition)

Identification of an individual from face-specific features. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Face perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-face-perception)

Recognition and processing of faces as a specialized perceptual category; N170-sensitive, FFA-dependent. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Gustatory perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-gustatory-perception)

Perception of taste qualities (sweet, sour, salty, bitter, umami). Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Motion perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-motion-perception)

Detection and interpretation of moving stimuli; includes global motion and optic flow. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Olfactory perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-olfactory-perception)

Perception of odor via the olfactory system. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Pattern recognition](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-pattern-recognition)

Categorization of sensory input according to its structure. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Somatosensory perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-somatosensory-perception)

Perception of bodily sensations, including touch, proprioception, temperature, and pain. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Visual form recognition](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-visual-form-recognition)

Assignment of visual input to a shape category prior to semantic identification. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Visual object recognition](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-visual-object-recognition)

Identification of objects from visual input, invariant over viewpoint, size, and lighting. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Visual perception](face_and_object_perception.md#hed-visual-perception)

Perception of visual information; includes form, motion, depth, color, and spatial layout. Engaged by 12 tasks.

## [Implicit and Statistical Learning](implicit_and_statistical_learning.md)

Acquisition of structure without explicit instruction: implicit memory, procedural memory. Paradigm-level coverage includes artificial grammar learning, serial reaction time, statistical word-segmentation, perceptual category learning — these live in `task_details.json`, not as separate process rows.

### [Implicit memory](implicit_and_statistical_learning.md#hed-implicit-memory)

Memory expressed without conscious recollection, measurable by priming, skill learning, and conditioning. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Procedural memory](implicit_and_statistical_learning.md#hed-procedural-memory)

Non-declarative memory for skills and procedures, expressed through improved performance rather than conscious recollection. Engaged by 5 tasks.

## [Inhibitory Control and Conflict Monitoring](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md)

Response inhibition, response conflict, conflict monitoring, interference control, executive attention, proactive control, reactive control, error detection, error correction.

### [Conflict monitoring](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-conflict-monitoring)

Detection of co-activation of incompatible response tendencies, proposed to recruit anterior cingulate cortex and trigger cognitive control. Engaged by 9 tasks.

### [Error correction](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-error-correction)

Adjustment of behavior following an error, including post-error slowing and improvement on subsequent trials. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Error detection](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-error-detection)

Recognition that a response was incorrect, indexed by the error-related negativity (ERN) and by error awareness. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Executive attention](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-executive-attention)

Resolution of conflict among thoughts, feelings, and responses; one of Posner's three attentional networks. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Interference control](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-interference-control)

Resistance to interference from task-irrelevant stimuli or competing response representations, measured by Stroop, Flanker, and Simon effects. Engaged by 9 tasks.

### [Proactive control](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-proactive-control)

Sustained, anticipatory maintenance of task goals that biases processing in preparation for an expected demand. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Reactive control](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-reactive-control)

Transient, stimulus-triggered engagement of control after interference or conflict is detected. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Response conflict](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-response-conflict)

Competing activation of two or more response representations on a single trial, typically engaging cognitive control. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Response inhibition](inhibitory_control_and_conflict_monitoring.md#hed-response-inhibition)

Suppression of a prepotent or already-initiated response when it becomes inappropriate, indexed behaviorally by stop-signal reaction time or commission errors. Engaged by 11 tasks.

## [Language Comprehension and Production](language_comprehension_and_production.md)

Mapping sound or text to meaning and meaning to articulation: lexical access, word recognition, reading, phonological processes, syntactic parsing, sentence comprehension, discourse processing, semantic processing, semantic knowledge, naming, verbal fluency, speech perception, speech production, language comprehension, language production.

### [Discourse processing](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-discourse-processing)

Integration of sentences into coherent representations of extended text or conversation. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Language comprehension](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-language-comprehension)

Extraction of meaning from linguistic input (spoken, written, or signed). Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Language production](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-language-production)

Generation of spoken, written, or signed linguistic output, from message to articulation. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Lexical access](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-lexical-access)

Retrieval of word-level representations (form and meaning) from memory. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Naming](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-naming)

Production of a word label for a presented stimulus (e.g., picture naming). Engaged by 1 task.

### [Phonological awareness](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-phonological-awareness)

Explicit awareness of the sound structure of spoken words (onsets, rimes, phonemes). Engaged by 1 task.

### [Phonological encoding](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-phonological-encoding)

Assembly of phonological representations during language production. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Reading](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-reading)

Visual processing of written text, integrating orthography, phonology, and meaning. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Semantic knowledge](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-semantic-knowledge)

Long-term store of facts, concepts, and word meanings. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Semantic processing](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-semantic-processing)

Access and integration of word and phrase meaning, indexed by the N400. Engaged by 8 tasks.

### [Sentence comprehension](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-sentence-comprehension)

Integration of lexical, syntactic, and semantic information to derive sentence meaning. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Speech perception](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-speech-perception)

Extraction of linguistic content from the acoustic speech signal, including phoneme categorization, word segmentation, and prosodic processing. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Speech production](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-speech-production)

Planning and articulation of spoken output; the motor component of articulation is covered under vocal-motor control in Motor Preparation, Timing, and Execution. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Syntactic parsing](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-syntactic-parsing)

Assignment of hierarchical grammatical structure to a linguistic input. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Verbal fluency](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-verbal-fluency)

Rapid generation of words under a semantic or phonemic constraint. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Word recognition](language_comprehension_and_production.md#hed-word-recognition)

Identification of a word as a lexical item from its perceptual input. Engaged by 3 tasks.

## [Long-Term Memory](long_term_memory.md)

Encoding, consolidation, retrieval; recall and recognition; recollection and familiarity; source memory; episodic, semantic, autobiographical, verbal memory; pattern completion and separation; proactive and retroactive interference; forgetting; directed forgetting; reconsolidation; prospective memory.

### [Autobiographical memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-autobiographical-memory)

Memory for personally experienced events across the lifespan, integrating episodic and semantic components. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Consolidation](long_term_memory.md#hed-consolidation)

Post-encoding stabilization of memory traces, dependent on time and often on sleep; includes synaptic and systems consolidation. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Declarative memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-declarative-memory)

Consciously accessible memory for facts and events; encompasses semantic and episodic memory. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Directed forgetting](long_term_memory.md#hed-directed-forgetting)

Reduced memory for items that have been cued to be forgotten, relative to items cued to be remembered. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Encoding](long_term_memory.md#hed-encoding)

Processes by which perceptual input is transformed into a memory representation at acquisition. Engaged by 13 tasks.

### [Episodic memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-episodic-memory)

Memory for specific events located in a particular place and time and accompanied by autonoetic consciousness. Engaged by 6 tasks.

### [Familiarity](long_term_memory.md#hed-familiarity)

Sense that a stimulus has been encountered before, in the absence of retrieval of contextual detail. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Forgetting](long_term_memory.md#hed-forgetting)

Loss of accessibility of previously encoded information, due to decay, interference, or retrieval failure. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Pattern completion](long_term_memory.md#hed-pattern-completion)

Retrieval of a complete memory from a partial or degraded cue; CA3 function. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Pattern separation](long_term_memory.md#hed-pattern-separation)

Transformation of similar input patterns into distinct, non-overlapping memory representations; dentate gyrus function. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Proactive interference](long_term_memory.md#hed-proactive-interference)

Disruption of new learning by previously learned material. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Prospective memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-prospective-memory)

Memory for intentions to act at a future time or on a future event. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Recall](long_term_memory.md#hed-recall)

Retrieval of items without an external cue provided at test (free or cued recall). Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Recognition](long_term_memory.md#hed-recognition)

Judgment that a test item has been previously encountered; supported by familiarity and recollection. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Recollection](long_term_memory.md#hed-recollection)

Retrieval of contextual detail about a prior event, including source information. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Reconsolidation](long_term_memory.md#hed-reconsolidation)

Destabilization and re-stabilization of a memory upon retrieval, creating a window for modification. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Retrieval](long_term_memory.md#hed-retrieval)

Reactivation of a stored memory representation; dissociable into cue-driven and strategic retrieval. Engaged by 14 tasks.

### [Retroactive interference](long_term_memory.md#hed-retroactive-interference)

Disruption of older memories by newly learned material. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Semantic memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-semantic-memory)

Long-term store of general knowledge about the world — facts, concepts, and word meanings — dissociable from episodic memory and supported by distributed cortical representations. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Source memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-source-memory)

Memory for the contextual origin of information (e.g., who said it, where it was seen). Engaged by 1 task.

### [Verbal memory](long_term_memory.md#hed-verbal-memory)

Memory for linguistic material (words, sentences), tested via word lists, story recall, and RAVLT. Engaged by 1 task.

## [Reasoning and Problem-Solving](reasoning_and_problem_solving.md)

Deductive, inductive, causal, analogical, mathematical reasoning; hypothesis testing; categorization; insight; means-ends analysis; planning; subgoaling.

### [Analogical reasoning](reasoning_and_problem_solving.md#hed-analogical-reasoning)

Mapping of relational structure from a source domain to a target domain. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Categorization](reasoning_and_problem_solving.md#hed-categorization)

Assignment of instances to categories according to their features. Engaged by 8 tasks.

### [Causal reasoning](reasoning_and_problem_solving.md#hed-causal-reasoning)

Inference about cause–effect relations from observation and intervention. Engaged by 1 task.

Inference in which the conclusion follows necessarily from the premises. Engaged by 1 task.

Generation and evaluation of alternative explanations against evidence. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Subgoaling](reasoning_and_problem_solving.md#hed-subgoaling)

Decomposition of a goal into an ordered sequence of subgoals whose satisfaction advances the overall task; dissociable from means-ends search and often studied through hierarchical task analysis. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Reward anticipation](reward_anticipation_and_motivation.md#hed-reward-anticipation)

Affective and neural response to cues predicting upcoming reward. Engaged by 4 tasks.

### [Reward consumption](reward_anticipation_and_motivation.md#hed-reward-consumption)

Affective and neural response to receipt of reward. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

## [Selective and Sustained Attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md)

Attention networks (alerting, orienting, executive attention); selective attention; sustained attention; attention shifting; attentional capture; divided attention; feature-based attention; object-based attention; spatial attention; temporal attention.

### [Alerting](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-alerting)

Achieving and maintaining a state of readiness to respond, whether tonic or phasic; one of Posner's three attentional networks. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Attention shifting](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-attention-shifting)

Reorienting of attention from one location, feature, or object to another, indexed by cost in RT or accuracy. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Attentional capture](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-attentional-capture)

Involuntary shift of attention to a salient stimulus that is not the current target of task-directed attention. Engaged by 4 tasks.

### [Divided attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-divided-attention)

Concurrent processing of two or more streams of information or two tasks, typically yielding dual-task interference. Engaged by 4 tasks.

### [Feature-based attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-feature-based-attention)

Selection of a specific visual feature (e.g., a color, orientation, or motion direction) that enhances processing of that feature throughout the visual field. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Object-based attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-object-based-attention)

Selection of an object as a unit of attention such that the attended features belonging to the object are processed together. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Orienting](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-orienting)

Selection of information from sensory input, typically in space, by covert or overt shifts of attention; one of Posner's three attentional networks. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Selective attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-selective-attention)

Prioritized processing of a task-relevant subset of stimuli (a location, feature, object, or modality) with suppression of irrelevant stimuli. Engaged by 22 tasks.

### [Spatial attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-spatial-attention)

Selection of a location in space for preferential sensory processing, measurable via cueing benefits and N2pc/P1 modulation. Engaged by 5 tasks.

### [Sustained attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-sustained-attention)

Maintenance of attentional engagement over extended intervals during which targets are rare or responses are repetitive; synonymous with vigilance. Engaged by 8 tasks.

### [Temporal attention](selective_and_sustained_attention.md#hed-temporal-attention)

Selection of a point in time for preferential processing, measurable via cueing benefits in time and the attentional blink. Engaged by 2 tasks.

## [Short-Term and Working Memory](short_term_and_working_memory.md)

Active maintenance, manipulation, working-memory updating, rehearsal, chunking; visual working memory; verbal working memory; spatial working memory; umbrella `hed_working_memory`.

### [Active maintenance](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-active-maintenance)

Holding information active over a delay without manipulation, through volitional, attention-demanding processes; a core subprocess of working memory. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Chunking](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-chunking)

Binding of multiple items into a single unit in memory to expand effective capacity. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Manipulation](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-manipulation)

Transformation of information held in working memory (reordering, reversing, combining) beyond simple maintenance. Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Rehearsal](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-rehearsal)

Covert repetition of material to refresh its representation in short-term memory, especially for verbal material (articulatory rehearsal). Engaged by 2 tasks.

### [Spatial working memory](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-spatial-working-memory)

Short-term storage of spatial locations and spatial relations. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Verbal working memory](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-verbal-working-memory)

Short-term storage of phonological/verbal information, historically "phonological loop"; supports digit span, Sternberg with letters. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Visual working memory](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-visual-working-memory)

Short-term storage and manipulation of visual information; capacity limited to ~3–4 items. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Working memory](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-working-memory)

System for the short-term maintenance, updating, and manipulation of task-relevant information over delays of seconds. Engaged by 7 tasks.

### [Working memory updating](short_term_and_working_memory.md#hed-working-memory-updating)

Replacement or revision of information currently held active in working memory with newly task-relevant content. One of three core executive functions in the Miyake et al. unity/diversity framework; decomposable into retrieval, transformation, and substitution subcomponents (Ecker et al.); implemented by a cortico-striatal gating mechanism that opens to admit new content and closes to protect maintenance. Distinct from long-term-memory reconsolidation (which occurs when retrieval triggers a prediction error and targets stored representations rather than the focus of attention). Indexed behaviorally by N-Back, running-span, and keep-track performance. Engaged by 3 tasks.

## [Value-Based Decision-Making Under Risk and Uncertainty](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md)

Choice among options that differ in subjective value: valuation, value-based decision making, choice commitment, delay discounting, intertemporal choice, probability judgment, risk processing.

### [Choice commitment](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-choice-commitment)

The act of locking in a selected option once accumulated evidence or value crosses a decision boundary; measured by response-time distributions, drift-diffusion boundary parameters, and motor preparation signatures. Engaged by no task in the current catalog.

### [Delay discounting](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-delay-discounting)

Devaluation of a reward as a function of the delay to its receipt, typically hyperbolic. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Intertemporal choice](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-intertemporal-choice)

Choice among options that differ in timing of outcomes; choice between smaller-sooner and larger-later. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Probability judgment](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-probability-judgment)

Estimation of likelihoods of events, often deviating from normative Bayes. Engaged by 1 task.

### [Risk processing](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-risk-processing)

Neural and behavioral evaluation of outcome uncertainty. Engaged by 3 tasks.

### [Valuation](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-valuation)

Assignment of a subjective value to a prospective option, integrating reward magnitude, probability, delay, and effort; indexed by willingness-to-pay, subjective ratings, and value-coded neural signals in vmPFC and striatum. Engaged by 4 tasks.

### [Value-based decision making](value_based_decision_making_under_risk_and_uncertainty.md#hed-value-based-decision-making)

Choice among options that differ in subjective value, computed across attributes. Engaged by 6 tasks.
