# Emotion Perception and Regulation

**Scope:** Recognition of emotional signals (face, voice, body), affective priming, cognitive reappraisal, expressive suppression, emotion regulation strategies.

**Out of scope:** Specific emotions as states (fear, happiness, disgust, anger, sadness — HED handles these as stimulus/state labels); affect as a trait; mood.

:::{note}
**Open issues:** Coverage of appraisal-stage processes is thin - expected to grow if paradigms that specifically target appraisal are added.
:::

This category contains 5 processes.

| Process | Definition | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| [Affective priming](#hed-affective-priming) | Facilitation or interference in evaluating a target by a valence-related prime. | 1 |
| [Cognitive reappraisal](#hed-cognitive-reappraisal) | Reinterpretation of an emotional stimulus to change its affective impact. | 1 |
| [Emotion recognition](#hed-emotion-recognition) | Identification of emotional states from cues such as facial expression or vocal prosody. | 7 |
| [Emotion regulation](#hed-emotion-regulation) | Processes by which individuals influence which emotions they have, when, and how they experience and express them. | 5 |
| [Expressive suppression](#hed-expressive-suppression) | Inhibition of outward behavioral expression of emotion. | 1 |

## Affective priming

**Process ID:** `hed_affective_priming`

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Affective Priming Task](../tasks/hedtsk_affective_priming.md)

(hed-emotion-recognition)=
## Emotion recognition

**Process ID:** `hed_emotion_recognition`

**Also known as:** **Emotion perception** - Broader term; recognition is the operationalized form (identification of a named emotion category).

Identification of emotional states from cues such as facial expression or vocal prosody.

**Tasks that engage this process:** [Affective Picture Viewing Task](../tasks/hedtsk_affective_picture_viewing.md), [Affective Priming Task](../tasks/hedtsk_affective_priming.md), [Dot-Probe Task](../tasks/hedtsk_dot_probe.md), [Emotion Regulation Task](../tasks/hedtsk_emotion_regulation.md), [Emotional Stroop Task](../tasks/hedtsk_emotional_stroop.md), [Facial Emotion Recognition Task](../tasks/hedtsk_facial_emotion_recognition.md), [Reading the Mind in the Eyes Task](../tasks/hedtsk_reading_the_mind_in_the_eyes.md)

**Fundamental references**

- Ekman & Friesen (1976) *Pictures of Facial Affect*
