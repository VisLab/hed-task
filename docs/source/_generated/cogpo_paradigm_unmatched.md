### Broad classes with no single counterpart

| CogPO paradigm class | CogPO definition | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Pitch Monitor/Discrimination Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Pitch_Monitor_or_Discrimination_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects are presented with various stimuli (human speech and non-speech vocalizations, animal vocalization, mechanical noise, etc.) and are instructed to listen to them passively (also co-coded with Passive Listening), or discriminate based on pitch (pleasant/unpleasant, same/different, duration, familiar/unfamiliar, male/female). | Pitch judgements on speech and non-speech sounds. The Catalog has no pitch-discrimination task. |
| [Spatial/Location Discrimination Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Spatial_or_Location_Discrimination_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects view shapes or other stimuli (letters, pictures, numbers, or arrows) and discriminate according to their location, orientation, or size. | Discrimination by location, orientation or size. A perceptual class with no Catalog counterpart. |
| [Tactile Monitor/Discrimination Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Tactile_Monitor_or_Discrimination_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects experience tactile/somatosensory stimulation and are asked to attend passively or discriminate according to some feature (shape, texture, same/different, frequency of presentation, etc.) Also includes: subjects are presented with 3-dimensional objects and are asked to manipulate them in their hands and probe their features. | Tactile discrimination. The Catalog has no somatosensory discrimination task; Body Ownership Illusion is tactile but tests ownership. |

### Physiological procedures and interventions

| CogPO paradigm class | CogPO definition | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Acupuncture Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Acupuncture_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which at least one of the conditions includes the application of acupuncture. | Acupuncture is an intervention, not a task; excluded by task criteria 1.2. |
| [Breath-Holding Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Breath-Holding_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions,  subjects hold their breath | Breath holding is a physiological challenge (vascular reactivity calibration), not a cognitive task. |
| [Chewing or Swallowing Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Chewing_or_Swallowing_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions,  subjects chew an oral stimulus that is not food (e.g., gum) or swallow their own saliva. If the oral stimulus is food or liquid that is swallowed, then the correct paradigm class is Eating/Drinking. | Oral motor act with no trial structure eliciting a cognitive process. |
| [Eating or Drinking Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Eating_or_Drinking_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects eat food (e.g., chocolate) or drink liquids (e.g., juice). | Consumption, not a task. |
| [Micturition Task Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Micturition_Task_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects void urine | Voiding; not a task. |
| [Non-Painful Electrical Stimulation Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Non-Painful_Electrical_Stimulation_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects are electrically stimulated at a non-painful threshold. | Somatosensory stimulation with no task; a stimulation procedure. |
| [Non-Painful Thermal Stimulation Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Non-Painful_Thermal_Stimulation_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects experience thermal stimulation (heat) at a non-painful threshold. | Thermal stimulation with no task; a stimulation procedure. |
| [Pain Monitor/Discrimination Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Pain_Monitor_or_Discrimination_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects experience thermal or electrical stimulation at a painful threshold. | Painful stimulation; a stimulation procedure, not a task. |
| [Sleep Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Sleep_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which in at least one condition subjects are instructed to sleep. | Instructed sleep; a state, not a task. On the CogPO wiki only. |
| [Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Transcranial_Magnetic_Stimulation_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects are stimulated with transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS). | Brain stimulation; an intervention, not a task. |
| [Vibrotactile Monitor/Discrimination Paradigm](http://www.wiki.cogpo.org/index.php?title=Vibrotactile_Monitor_or_Discrimination_Paradigm) | A Behavioral Experimental Paradigm in which, in at least one of the conditions, Subjects experience vibrotactile stimulation to the hand, finger, arm, toe, or lip. | Vibrotactile stimulation with no task; a stimulation procedure. |
